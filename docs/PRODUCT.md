# Product

## What this is

A single page that does one job: when someone opens it after reading Kevin's LinkedIn or CV, they should be able to judge the work in under a minute and want to talk to him.

## Who opens it

Hiring managers and engineers at remote-first product companies, mostly in payments and fintech, screening for Staff or Principal individual contributor roles. Recruiters land here second and scan for the same things.

## What success looks like

- The four numbers are legible in the first viewport.
- A reader can name what he built, at what scale, and what it changed, without scrolling twice.
- No claim on the page requires an explanation Kevin cannot give in an interview.
- It prints as a clean CV without a second layout.

## Not this

- Not a blog. Writing may come later and it does not shape the page now.
- Not a museum of the 2015 site. The old timeline, flag sprites and skill table with invented levels are gone.
- Not a visual experiment at the cost of reading. The design is quiet and the numbers carry the weight.

## Content rules

- Every number is defensible, with the same wording as the CV and LinkedIn.
- Sentence case for roles, capitals for names, products and acronyms.
- No adjectives about himself that the work does not show.
- Dates and employers match the CV exactly.
